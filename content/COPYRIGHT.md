## What this policy covers ("Case Content")

- story premises, plots, and the hidden "what really happened" narratives;
- characters, their names, biographies, and dialogue (statements and reactions);
- evidence text (documents, letters, records, transcripts) and summaries;
- hint text, contradiction explanations, solutions, answer keys, and red-herring explanations;
- original artwork, photographs, and other media created for Casefile;
- the case narrative excerpts reproduced in `docs/CASE_DESIGN.md` §9–10 (*The Missing Masterpiece*).
